Strategic Foresight Is Preparation, Not Prediction

The practical difference between predicting the future and preparing intelligently for multiple possible futures.

foresight future strategy

Foresight is not a promise that tomorrow can be known. It is a discipline for identifying signals, constructing plausible scenarios and preparing decisions that remain useful under uncertainty.
