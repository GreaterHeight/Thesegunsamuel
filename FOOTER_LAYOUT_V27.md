# Footer Layout V27

The global footer now treats all five navigation groups as first-class columns: EXPLORE, THE WORK, KNOWLEDGE, PARTICIPATE, and LEGAL & PRIVACY. The brand occupies the first column. This prevents LEGAL & PRIVACY from wrapping underneath the brand at desktop widths.

Desktop: brand + five navigation columns.
Medium: brand spans the full first row, navigation groups flow in three columns.
Mobile: single-column stack.
