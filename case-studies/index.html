Reality-Tested Thinking

Case Studies that turn observation into insight.

A case study examines reality through the SegunSamuel architecture: observe what happened, identify the pattern, interpret the underlying structure, apply a framework, design a response and extract a transferable lesson.

The Method

Reality → Observation → Pattern → Interpretation → Framework → Action → Lesson

This is deliberately different from a conventional article. The objective is not simply to tell a story. It is to develop the reader's capacity to see and reason.

Observe

Separate what happened from what people assume happened.

Interpret

Identify relationships, incentives, patterns and context.

Design

Use an appropriate framework to structure the response.

Apply

Extract a lesson that transfers to another situation.
