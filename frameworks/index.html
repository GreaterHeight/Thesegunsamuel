02 — How Frameworks Work

One idea becomes more useful when it can travel.

A framework should not remain trapped on a page. It can become a lens in an article, a method in a masterclass, a diagnostic in consulting, a principle in mentoring, a subject in a podcast or a decision architecture inside an organisation.
