The Reading List

Recommended books.

Selected books that sharpen the thinking behind strategy, systems, human behaviour, leadership and disciplined action.

No books match your current search and filters.

The Laws of Human Nature
Robert Greene's study of the hidden drivers behind human behaviour — envy, grandiosity, conformity — and how to master them in yourself and others. The Purpose Driven Life
Rick Warren's 40-day devotional answering the question every leader eventually asks: what on earth am I here for? Games People Play
Eric Berne's classic on transactional analysis — the hidden scripts and psychological games people run in relationships without realising it. Boundaries
Cloud and Townsend on when to say yes and how to say no — a Christian counselling framework for protecting your time, energy, and relationships. The 48 Laws of Power
Robert Greene's compendium of power dynamics through history — tactics distilled from statesmen, generals, and con artists alike. Black Book of Power
Stan Taylor's field manual on personal power — direct, unflinching tactics for command, influence, and self-mastery in high-stakes environments. Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion
Robert Cialdini's landmark study of the six principles that make people say yes — essential reading for anyone in marketing or leadership. The Dictator's Handbook
Bueno de Mesquita and Smith's cynical, data-driven account of how leaders really stay in power — essential reading on political survival. Mastery
Robert Greene on the path from apprenticeship to true mastery — patience, deep observation, and the discipline that separates experts from amateurs. The Art of War
Sun Tzu's 2,500-year-old treatise on strategy, deception, and positioning — still the foundational text of military and competitive thinking. The Book of Five Rings
Miyamoto Musashi's treatise on swordsmanship and strategy, written from a cave in 1645 — timeless lessons on discipline and decisive action. The Behavior Ops Manual
A tactical guide to reading behaviour and neuro-cognitive cues — practical intelligence for negotiation, leadership, and high-stakes decision-making. The 77 Masks of Human Power
Seventy-seven archetypes of influence and control, examined for the leader who wants to recognise power plays before they land. The Black Book of Strategic War
A concentrated field manual on strategic warfare — doctrine, deception, and decisive positioning drawn from history's most consequential campaigns. Meditations
Marcus Aurelius's private journal of Stoic philosophy — discipline, mortality, and duty, written by a Roman emperor governing an empire. The Prince
Machiavelli's blunt handbook for acquiring and holding power — the founding text of political realism, still debated five centuries later. In Sheep's Clothing
George Simon's guide to recognising manipulative and covert-aggressive people — how to spot the tactics before you become their target. The Christian in Complete Armour
William Gurnall's Puritan classic on spiritual warfare — Ephesians 6 expounded at length, on standing firm against unseen opposition. Algorithms to Live By
Christian and Griffiths translate computer science into everyday decisions — search, scheduling, and uncertainty made practical for real life. The 33 Strategies of War
Robert Greene's catalogue of offensive and defensive strategy drawn from history's great campaigns — applied to the battles of everyday life. Psychology of Intelligence Analysis
Richards Heuer's CIA-honed framework for structured thinking — how trained analysts avoid the cognitive traps that distort judgment. Thinking in Systems
Donella Meadows's primer on systems thinking — feedback loops, leverage points, and why complex problems resist simple fixes. The Logic of Failure
Dietrich Dörner on why intelligent people make catastrophic decisions in complex situations — a study in systems thinking gone wrong. The 77 Laws of Great Leaders
Piseth Phon's study of stable, quiet leadership — why calm defeats panic and consistency builds more trust than speeches. The Coaching Habit
Michael Bungay Stanier's practical guide to asking better questions — seven habit-forming questions that make you a sharper coach and leader. White Witchcraft vs Dark Psychology
Selena Blackwood's guide to spiritual protection and mental clarity — recognising manipulation and reclaiming your energy from toxic influence. Discourses on Livy
Machiavelli's lesser-known but arguably deeper work — republics, civic virtue, and the strategic lessons of Roman history applied to statecraft. Complete Works of Carl Jung
A comprehensive collection of Jung's foundational writings — psychological types, the unconscious, and the architecture of analytical psychology. The Undiscovered Self
Jung's late, urgent essay on the individual's place within mass society — a warning against losing yourself to the collective. Systematic Theology
Wayne Grudem's exhaustive introduction to biblical doctrine — the standard reference for serious study of Christian theology, chapter by chapter.
