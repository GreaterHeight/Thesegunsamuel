The Proposition

Do not separate what was meant to work together.

TECHSPIBUS™ is the established name for Technology × Spirituality × Business in the Segun Samuel ecosystem. It is expressed through technology, education, publishing and marketplace application rather than kept as an abstract idea.

The existing work describes the three dimensions as connected assignments: GreaterHeight carries the technology and education lanes; TECHSPIBUS™ carries publishing and intellectual property; the marketplace remains the place where ideas encounter reality.

Three Dimensions

Tools. Architecture. Proof.

Technology

Build the infrastructure.

Technology supplies the tools through which strategic intent becomes software, data, systems and operating capability.

Spirituality

Hold the architecture.

Spiritual conviction and Scripture provide the architecture through which the work is interpreted, disciplined and directed.

Business

Enter the arena.

Business is the arena of proof: conviction encounters numbers, customers, decisions, risk, responsibility and consequence.

A Working Principle

Ideas should survive contact with reality.

“Technology supplies the tools and Scripture supplies the architecture, but business is where conviction is audited.”

This proposition already appears in the ecosystem's published work on dominion and stewardship. TECHSPIBUS™ therefore does not collapse spirituality into business or business into spirituality; it refuses to divorce them.
