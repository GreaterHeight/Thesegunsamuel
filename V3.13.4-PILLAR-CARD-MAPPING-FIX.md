# v3.13.4 — Pillar Card Mapping Fix

The v3.13.3 pillar pages incorrectly filtered cards by requiring a route.
That caused a valid pillar such as LEARN to display `0 available experiences`
even though the Knowledge Graph contained mapped entities.

v3.13.4 fixes this by:

- rendering every entity mapped to a pillar;
- keeping `OPEN →` where a canonical route exists;
- showing `VIEW CONNECTIONS →` where an entity has no standalone route;
- preserving search/filter behavior;
- preserving the Knowledge Graph as the source of ecosystem relationships.

Mapped entities in this build:
- LEARN: 3
- THINK: 55
- APPLY: 3
- LISTEN: 1

No canonical entity data was deleted or relocated.
