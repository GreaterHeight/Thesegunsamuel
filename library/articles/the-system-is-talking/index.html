The System Is Talking

Recurring problems as structural signals

ArticleSystems & Strategic ArchitectureSegunSamuel Intellectual Library
The Thinking

A recurring problem is often a message from the system that produces it. If a failure repeatedly appears despite competent people trying to fix it, inspect incentives, workflows, information flow, ownership, constraints and feedback. The goal is to stop treating every recurrence as an isolated incident. Sometimes the better question is not 'Who failed?' but 'What design keeps producing this outcome?'
