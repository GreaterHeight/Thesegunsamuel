Dark Psychology as a Lens

Examining manipulation, control and shadow behaviour

ArticleHuman Behaviour & PowerSegunSamuel Intellectual Library
The Thinking

The study of manipulation, control, power dynamics and shadow behaviour can become useful when treated as a lens rather than an identity. Within this system, dark psychology helps examine how human behaviour interacts with leadership, relationships, institutions and strategy. The emphasis is on recognition, discernment and responsible understanding—not on teaching people to exploit others. This lens belongs inside the wider study of human systems.
