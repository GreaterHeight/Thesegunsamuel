Power Beneath the Surface

The invisible forces that shape behaviour

ArticleHuman Behaviour & PowerSegunSamuel Intellectual Library
The Thinking

Power is often easiest to miss when it does not look like command. It can operate through access, incentives, dependency, information, reputation, status and the ability to define what others consider possible. Understanding these dynamics is useful for leadership because organisations are human systems before they are charts and procedures. The objective is not manipulation. It is discernment: seeing the forces that shape behaviour so that decisions can be made with greater clarity.
