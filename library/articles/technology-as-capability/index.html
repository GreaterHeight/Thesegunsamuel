Technology Is Capability

A better way to think about AI and data

ArticleAI & Digital IntelligenceSegunSamuel Intellectual Library
The Thinking

AI, data and software are not valuable merely because they are modern. Their strategic value comes from what they allow people and organisations to see, decide, automate, create or coordinate that was previously difficult or impossible. Technology should therefore be evaluated as capability: what human or institutional capacity does it expand?
