Patterns Before Predictions

Why strategic foresight begins with interpretation

ArticleStrategic ForesightSegunSamuel Intellectual Library
The Thinking

Prediction asks what will happen. Foresight asks what could happen, what signals point toward different possibilities, and what we should prepare to do. Pattern recognition strengthens foresight because isolated events rarely explain themselves. Relationships, recurring behaviours and structural changes often reveal more than a single headline. Strategic foresight therefore begins with disciplined interpretation of the present.
