Mental Models for Strategic Judgment

Principles for thinking under complexity

ArticlePhilosophy & Mental ModelsSegunSamuel Intellectual Library
The Thinking

Complex environments make complete information impossible. Mental models provide working structures for reasoning when certainty is unavailable. A good model does not replace reality. It helps us interrogate reality, expose assumptions and recognise relationships that might otherwise remain invisible. Strategic judgment improves when models are used as instruments rather than treated as unquestionable truth.
