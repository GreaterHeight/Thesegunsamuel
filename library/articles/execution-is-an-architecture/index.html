Execution Is an Architecture

Why good strategy still fails

ArticleExecution IntelligenceSegunSamuel Intellectual Library
The Thinking

Execution is frequently reduced to effort. But durable execution depends on architecture. There must be a clear outcome, sequence, ownership, resources, feedback loops and mechanisms for correction. Without these, even intelligent strategy can remain trapped at the level of intention. Execution intelligence asks how an idea survives contact with reality.
