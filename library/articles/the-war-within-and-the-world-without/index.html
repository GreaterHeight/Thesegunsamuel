The War Within and the World Without

How inner architecture affects outer execution

ArticleThe Architect WithinSegunSamuel Intellectual Library
The Thinking

The quality of outer execution is influenced by inner architecture. Attention, identity, assumptions, fears and unresolved contradictions affect how reality is interpreted and how decisions are made. The inner and outer worlds are not identical, but they interact. Developing capability therefore requires both disciplined external design and honest internal examination.
