How to actually earn with this

Promote the ideas, not the product.

Affiliate work fails when it becomes link dumping. It works when the recommendation arrives inside a useful conversation: a passage, a case, a book, a course, a podcast discussion, a teaching note or a real problem your audience is already trying to solve.

What converts: highlighted passages, thoughtful reviews, honest comparisons, relevant excerpts, podcast conversations, YouTube breakdowns, newsletters and social threads that help people understand why the work matters.

The programme rewards qualified referrals. It does not authorise misleading claims, spam, fake endorsements, trademark misuse or paid promotion that has not been approved.
