FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions before you begin.

Who is this course for?

The course is designed for learners whose goals match the stated course level, outcomes and application focus.

How is the course delivered?

The catalogue identifies the programme as an online learning experience. Specific live-session and assessment arrangements are stated in enrolment information.

Will I receive a certificate?

Where the course is marked certificate-bearing, successful completion qualifies the learner to earn the stated certificate, subject to completion requirements.

Can I continue into another learning experience?

Yes. Courses connect into learning paths, frameworks, masterclasses, case studies and applications.
