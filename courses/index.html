Courses · Capability Development

Learn to see, think, design, execute and build.

SegunSamuel courses are structured capability-building experiences. Technology, AI, data, leadership, strategy, foresight, systems, philosophy and human behaviour are different expressions of a larger purpose: developing people who can interpret reality and act deliberately.

Course Architecture

Every course has a job to do.

A course exists when knowledge can be transformed into a structured capability that can be learned, practiced and demonstrated.

Learn

Understand the concepts and framework.

Practice

Work through guided exercises and cases.

Build

Create a tangible project or portfolio artefact.

Apply

Transfer capability into real work.
