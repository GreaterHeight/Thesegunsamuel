SHORT BIO · 100 WORDS · INTRODUCTIONS & PROGRAMMES

Segun Samuel is a Lagos-based writer, strategist, technologist and marketplace architect working at the intersection of Technology, Spirituality, and Business. He is the founder and CEO of GreaterHeight Technologies and the creator of TECHSPIBUS™, an integrating framework for technology, conviction and enterprise. His work examines strategic intelligence, leadership, identity, systems and the psychological forces beneath consequential decisions. He is the author of The War Within and creator of The Prepared Mind Method™, a strategic planning discipline built around counting the cost before committing. His work is designed for leaders and builders who must see reality clearly and build what should exist next.

EXTENDED BIO · 250 WORDS · FEATURES & PROFILES

Segun Samuel is a Marketplace Architect, Data Polymath and Strategic Foresight Mentor based in Lagos, Nigeria. His work sits at the intersection of technology, spirituality and business — three domains he treats not as separate compartments, but as one integrated field of responsibility.

He leads GreaterHeight Technologies, an IT consulting and software development firm working across digital infrastructure, software engineering and data systems. He also builds intellectual and learning platforms around strategic thinking, leadership, planning and the human forces beneath consequential decisions.

His intellectual system includes TECHSPIBUS™, The Prepared Mind Method™, PLAN and IBONK™, alongside the wider body of work developed through The War Within, which examines seventeen internal battles leaders face across identity, purpose, fear, comparison, discipline and relationships.

His work is ultimately concerned with integration: seeing reality clearly, interpreting what is seen, thinking strategically, designing intelligently, executing deliberately, building systems and creating institutions that endure. He speaks, writes, mentors and consults for leaders, organisations and audiences navigating consequential change.
