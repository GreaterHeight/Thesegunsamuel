Data Processing

Processing categories

  • Enquiries and contact information
  • Newsletter subscriptions
  • Consent records
  • Technical/security information
  • Future course enrolment and payment records

Processing principles

Collect what is needed, use it for stated purposes, protect it, retain it appropriately and honour applicable data-subject rights.

Processors and transfers

Hosting, email, analytics, payments and authentication processors should be documented and assessed before production.
