What this is

Session Architecture

Understand → Interrogate → Apply → Reflect

The intensive is designed to create movement, not simply information.

01 — Understand

Establish the framework, vocabulary and central problem.

02 — Interrogate

Work through cases, questions, tensions and competing interpretations.

03 — Apply

Use the capability on a real situation, decision or design challenge.

04 — Reflect

Capture lessons, assumptions and next actions.
