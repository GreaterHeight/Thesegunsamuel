The Proposition

Consulting begins where the presenting problem ends.

The visible problem is rarely the whole problem. Serious advisory work starts by understanding the reality beneath it—then designing the appropriate response.

01 — The Work

Diagnose the system before prescribing the solution.

Organisations often respond to symptoms with activity: another initiative, another meeting, another tool, another strategy document. But performance is shaped by the architecture underneath—structures, incentives, information, people, technology, power and execution.

The consulting work is to make that architecture visible, identify the leverage points and design what the organisation actually needs next.

02 — Advisory Architecture

Six domains. One strategic lens.

Engagements can enter through strategy, systems, intelligence, technology, leadership or institution building. The domains are distinct, but the problems are often connected.

Strategy

Strategic Architecture

Clarify direction, choices, assumptions, constraints and the architecture required to execute.

Systems

Systems & Organisational Architecture

Examine structures, processes, incentives, information flows and operating mechanisms behind performance.

Intelligence

Strategic Intelligence & Foresight

Interpret signals, patterns, uncertainty and possible futures to improve strategic judgement.

Data · AI

AI, Data & Decision Systems

Translate data and AI capability into useful decision systems, workflows and organisational capability.

Leadership

Leadership & Power Dynamics

Examine behaviour, incentives, influence, power and the human forces shaping execution.

Institutions

Institution Building

Design for continuity, institutional memory, governance and systems that can outlive individual founders.
