# V102 — Case Studies + Applications Visual Reconstruction

## Scope
Visual reconstruction of `/case-studies/` and `/application/` from the V101 architecture baseline.

## Strategy
- Case Studies is treated as the ecosystem's examination/diagnostic layer.
- Applications is treated as the applied intervention/project layer.
- Speaking remains a distinct professional-output experience.
- Existing page copy, case/application inventory, routes and detail pages are preserved.

## Visual changes
- Full-width editorial hero treatment with architectural grid texture.
- Stronger typographic scale and hierarchy.
- Distinct method/architecture sections.
- Refined card system with editorial metadata, accent rail and hover elevation.
- Stronger library/search presentation.
- Dark bridge treatment and responsive refinements.
- No new image assets or external stock images introduced.

## Image prompt impact
No new image slots were introduced in V102; therefore no new image-generation prompts are required.
